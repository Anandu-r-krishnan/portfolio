About me

I’m Anandu — a video editor, cinematographer and colourist working between Bengaluru and Kochi.

I work closely with each client to understand their tone and intention first. Whether it’s a brand film, a short story or a documentary, the mood gets built for that project rather than pulled off a preset shelf.

Recent work includes campaigns for Lexus, Skoda, MakeMyTrip, Rare Rabbit and Sid’s Farm, and a reel that crossed 33 million views — around thirty times the creator’s previous best.

Story first, not just clean

I don’t just cut clips — I shape emotion and rhythm. Pacing, sound and tone always serve the narrative before they serve the technique.

A defined colour identity

Every frame I touch has a mood behind it. I build custom palettes and LUTs so each film ends up with a look of its own.

Both sides of the frame

Because I shoot and edit, I know what gets captured on set and how it has to live in the cut. Neither decision is made blind.

Disciplined, but not rigid

Colour-managed workflows in Resolve and Fusion underneath, an intuitive and organic process on top.

Get in touch

Tell me what you’re making, roughly when, and the tone you’re after. I’ll come back to you within a day.
